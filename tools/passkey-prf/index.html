Passkey PRF test

Postbox plans to lock your mail password with a key that only your passkey can produce (the WebAuthn "PRF" extension). Not every browser and passkey provider supports it. This page finds out whether yours does. Nothing leaves this page.

  1. Press Create a passkey and save it where you normally keep passkeys. You are asked twice: once to create it, once to use it.
  2. Press Sign in with it and pick the passkey named "prf-test".
  3. Copy the result box and send it back. Then delete the "prf-test" passkey from your provider.
