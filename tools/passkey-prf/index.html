Passkey PRF test

Postbox plans to lock your mail password with a key that only your passkey can produce (the WebAuthn "PRF" extension). Not every browser and passkey provider supports it. This page finds out whether yours does. Nothing leaves this page.

  1. Press Create a passkey and save it where you normally keep passkeys.
  2. Press Use it and approve.
  3. Press Sign in with it and pick the newest passkey named "prf-test".
  4. Copy the result box and send it back. Then delete the "prf-test" passkeys from your provider.
